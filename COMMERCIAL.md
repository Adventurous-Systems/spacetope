spacetope is dual-licensed.

- **AGPL-3.0-or-later** (`LICENSE`). Free to use, study, modify and share. If you distribute spacetope, or
  a program built on it, or let people use a modified version over a network, you must offer them the
  complete corresponding source code under the same licence.
- **Commercial licence.** For use in closed-source products or hosted services without the AGPL's
  obligations. Granted by Adventurous Systems, the copyright holder.

Versions up to and including commit `c419c33` (2026-10-16) were released under Apache-2.0 and remain
available under it.

## What a commercial licence does not cover

Third-party components keep their own licences (`NOTICE`). In particular, `topologic_core`, the binary
kernel topologicpy falls back to, is AGPL-3.0 upstream and is not owned by this project. A commercial
licence of spacetope does not license it; a closed build must use a kernel without it.
